Measurement source

Enter values from your team’s PWM step test and encoder log. These editable values start as a demonstration model. They become project evidence only after you replace them with your measurements.

K = change in steady speed / change in PWM. τ is the time to reach 63.2% of that speed change after the input step.

PI design & verification

Match τs² + (1+KKp)s + KKi to the chosen second-order characteristic polynomial. Kp = (2ζωnτ − 1)/K and Ki = τωn²/K. The PI zero also influences the output, so the measured overshoot must be checked independently.

Chosen demonstration spec: overshoot ≤10%, settling time ≤3 s, error ≤1%, PWM within 0–100%. These limits aim to keep a Micromouse speed loop responsive without large speed excursions. Replace the target and limits if your measured robot needs different behavior.

Pin 9 supplies PWM. Fill readSpeed() using your encoder scale and sample interval. Speed units must match the model. This is an integration template until the encoder function is connected.
